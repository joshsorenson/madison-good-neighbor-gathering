Good to know

🎉 It's free

No tickets, no registration, no wristbands. Walk in and enjoy it.

🍔 Food carts

Cash and card both work. There's no ATM on site, so bring cash if that's your plan. Picnics are welcome too.

♿ Getting around

Paved paths throughout the park, good for strollers, wheelchairs and mobility devices.

🐶 Dogs

Welcome on a leash. Please clean up after them.

🌞 Out in the open

It's a park with limited shade. Water and sunscreen go a long way on a sunny afternoon.

📷 Photos

Snap away, and tag @madisongoodneighborgathering so we can see it.
