Parking

Lot full? There’s plenty of free parking a short walk away.

Good news: it’s a Sunday

Madison lifts its 2‑hour limits and residential permit rules on Sundays and city holidays. So nearly every street around Brittingham Park is free, unlimited parking during the Gathering. Park anywhere there isn’t a no‑parking sign, and stay as long as you like.

  • ✅ Permit blocks are open today. Those “RP3 / 2 HR, 8A–6P” signs all end with Except Sun., so they don’t apply.
  • 🚫 Still off limits: anything marked No Parking Any Time, bus stops, alley mouths and corners.
  • ♿ Dropping someone off? The pavilion sits closest to the W Washington Ave side of the park, and the paths in are paved and flat.
  • 🚌 Taking the bus? Metro Transit stops at S Park & W Washington, about a 3 minute walk from the pavilion.
  • 🚲 Biking is easiest. Free bike racks at the park. The nearest BCycle stations are about a 6 minute walk: W Washington & Regent, or S Mills & Mound.
