Parking

There’s plenty of free parking a short walk away.

  • ♿ Dropping someone off? W Washington Ave and S Park St both run right along the park, and the paths in are paved and flat.
  • 🚌 Taking the bus? Metro Transit stops at S Park & W Washington, right at the edge of the park.
  • 🚲 Biking is easiest. Free bike racks at the park, and BCycle stations are 2–3 minutes away at W Washington & Regent or S Mills & Mound.
