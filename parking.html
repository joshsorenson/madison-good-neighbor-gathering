Two things to know

🚫

Please skip the Brittingham Park lot

It is small, and on event day it is doing a different job:

  • Vendor and food cart load-in
  • Performer and equipment drop-off
  • Accessible parking for visitors with a disability permit

Everyone else: the street is a two minute walk and always free.

🤝

Volunteers park at New Culture Church

301 S Bedford St, Madison, WI 53703

  • Park there, then walk over to the park
  • Roughly a 7–9 minute walk, all flat and paved
  • Carpool if you can — it keeps the close spots open for families

Parking questions

Does it cost anything?
No. Street parking around Brittingham Park is free on Sundays. Read the signs on your block anyway — a few streets closer to downtown carry posted time limits.
How early should I get there?
If it is sunny, aim to arrive by 12:15 PM for a close spot. After that, head straight for Zone 3 or Zone 4 instead of circling. The walk is short and the extra five minutes beats ten minutes of looping.
Is there accessible parking?
Yes. Accessible spaces in the Brittingham Park lot off W Washington Ave stay open for visitors with a disability parking permit. Brittingham Park has paved paths throughout, so strollers, wheelchairs and mobility devices get around easily.
Can I drop people off first?
Absolutely. Pull over on W Washington Ave, let everyone out, then go find a spot. It is the easiest way to handle strollers, coolers and blankets.
I am volunteering. Where do I go?
New Culture Church, 301 S Bedford St. Park there and walk over — about 7 to 9 minutes. Leaving the closest street spots for families is one of the most useful things a volunteer does all day.
What about bikes?
Bring them. The Capital City Trail runs along Monona Bay right past the park, BCycle docking stations are close by, and there is plenty of room to lock up.
