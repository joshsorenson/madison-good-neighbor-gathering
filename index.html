Plan Your Visit

What to pack, and how to get there.

🧺 What to Bring

  • ✓ Blanket or lawn chairs for seating
  • ✓ Sunscreen & sunglasses
  • ✓ Water bottle
  • ✓ Cash or card for food carts
♿ Fully accessible. Paved paths for strollers & wheelchairs throughout Brittingham Park.

📍 Getting There

  • 🚗 Free street parking on the surrounding streets. Arrive 15 minutes early on sunny days.
  • 🚌 Several Metro Transit routes stop nearby, and BCycle docks are a short ride away.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Good Neighbor Gathering really free?
Yes. No tickets, no registration, no hidden fees. Just show up.
When is the 2027 Gathering?
The 2027 date hasn’t been announced yet. It lands on a Sunday afternoon in early fall, and it has run 12:00 to 4:00 PM in past years. Follow us on Instagram and we’ll post the date as soon as it’s set.
What about severe weather?
The event is only cancelled in the case of severe weather. Check our Instagram for any updates.
Is there parking nearby?
Free street parking surrounds the park, and it fills up on nice days. Metro Transit and BCycle are both good alternatives. See the parking map →
Can I bring my dog?
Yes, on a leash. Clean up after them and keep them comfortable in the crowd.
How can I volunteer or sponsor the event?
Head to the Get Involved page. Volunteers and sponsors are what keep it free.
