How to print this

  • Built at 24 × 36 in portrait — the standard A-frame insert size. For a 22 × 28 in board, change @page size and the .sheet width/height at the top of this file; everything else rescales.
  • Print two copies so both faces of the A-frame match.
  • Print to PDF at 100% scale (no “fit to page”), then send to the printer. Ask for weatherproof stock if it sits outside.
  • Scan the QR with a phone after printing, before the sign goes out. It points to madisongoodneighborgathering.org/parking.html
